# 🤝 Contributing to Recallspection

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Recallspection: the cryptographic exact memory layer for AGI & AI agents.

This project is a formal research initiative of Sciencedelic Metatech under Principal Investigator Eliam Raell. Contributions are subject to both technical standards and legal governance.

---

## 📋 Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

All contributions must be accompanied by a **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)**.

By signing off your commits, you certify that:

1.  You have the right to submit the contribution.
2.  The contribution is your original work, or you have permission to submit it.
3.  You understand that the contribution is licensed under the AGPLv3 (or commercial license, where applicable).
4.  You grant the project and its users the right to use, modify, and distribute your contribution under the project's license.

**To sign off:** Add `Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>` to your commit message.
